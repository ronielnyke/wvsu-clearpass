Workflow

How it works

A simple four-step clearance process.

Log in

Access your account as a student or office staff.

Submit

Check requirements and upload your documents.

Review

Offices approve or reject each submission with remarks.

Completed

Once all offices approve, “Clearance Completed” appears.
